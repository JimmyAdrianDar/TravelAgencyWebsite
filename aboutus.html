About Dreamscape

Dreamscape Travel & Tour Co. is a travel agency specializing in Philippine beach and island tour packages. We make traveling easier by providing tour planning and complete travel arrangements—from budget-friendly trips to full luxury experiences.

01

WHO WE ARE

Dreamscape Travel & Tours Co. is a full-service travel agency dedicated to making every journey easy, meaningful, and unforgettable.

02

WHAT WE OFFER

We offer personalized travel planning, dedicated translation assistance, and all-inclusive beach packages designed to make every Philippine getaway smooth, enjoyable, and worry-free.

03

WHO WE SERVE

We serve Filipino and foreign travelers aged 18 and above, including solo travelers, couples, families, friends, students, and young professionals who love exploring the beaches and islands of the Philippines.

OUR PURPOSE

WHAT DRIVES US

01

OUR MISSION

To create travel itineraries based on clients’ requests and help them learn about their chosen destinations. We arrange comfortable trips with the services and products included in each package, so clients can enjoy their journey without worrying about where to go, stay, or how to get there.

02

OUR VISION

To become a globally trusted agency that partners with businesses to provide travel packages based on clients’ preferences. We aim to grow among the Philippines’ leading travel agencies while helping showcase the country’s unique heritage and tourism experiences.

03

OUR GOAL

To build strong connections with clients by providing excellent service and arranging itineraries that turn their travel preferences into reality. We aim to maintain high service standards and become one of the travel agencies known for customer satisfaction.

04

OUR OBJECTIVES

To create packages that match clients’ preferences and exceed their expectations, provide comfortable and wholesome experiences, promote sustainable tourism, and build partnerships that help the business grow.

OUR BUSINESS STRUCTURE

General Partnership

Dreamscape Travel & Tour Co. is owned and managed by eight partners who work together to build and operate the business. Each partner contributes skills, shares responsibilities, participates in decision-making, and receives a fair share of the profits.
