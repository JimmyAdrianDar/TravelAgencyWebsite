About Dreamscape

Dreamscape Travel & Tour Co. is a travel agency specializing in Philippine beach and island tour packages. We make traveling easier by providing tour planning and complete travel arrangements—from budget-friendly trips to full luxury experiences.

01

WHO WE ARE

Dreamscape Travel & Tours Co. is a full-service travel agency dedicated to making every journey easy, meaningful, and unforgettable.

02

WHAT WE OFFER

We offer personalized travel planning, dedicated translation assistance, and all-inclusive beach packages designed to make every Philippine getaway smooth, enjoyable, and worry-free.

03

WHO WE SERVE

We serve Filipino and foreign travelers aged 18 and above, including solo travelers, couples, families, friends, students, and young professionals who love exploring the beaches and islands of the Philippines.
